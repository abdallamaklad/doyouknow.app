Direction A · Conservative

Refined System

Keeps today's structure and the navy + amber identity. Commits the homepage to Explore only enough to add search and a real category rail — the card grid stays. Everything else is correctness work: the documented type scale finally becomes tokens, category colour finally reaches the badges, contrast and hit targets reach AA.

Fixes
Type scale, contrast, hit targets, sort order, "General" badges, title-in-image, RTL logical properties
Leaves
Composition largely as-is — still a card grid over a masthead
Type cost
None. Inter + Cairo/Tajawal, already loaded
Risk
Lowest. Ships fastest; least differentiated — competent, not distinctive
Home · above the fold

Search and category filters appear above the fold — the minimum an Explore surface owes a 361-article library. The dead newsletter block and the decorative gradient panel are gone.

Direction B · Strong fit

Editorial Index

Commits fully to Explore. The homepage stops being a landing page and becomes an index: a compact masthead, search, category rail, then a dense text-led list where the headline does the work and the thumbnail is a 96px aid. Amber stops being a fill and becomes a rule — a mark of structure. A serif display face gives the knowledge brand a voice that Inter alone cannot.

Composition
Rows, not tiles. Roughly 3× more articles visible per screen
Identity
Serif headlines + naskh Arabic; amber as rule/marker, navy as ink
Type cost
One display family, 2 weights, subset — budget ~30–45KB. Arabic naskh adds ~40KB if adopted
Risk
Moderate. Biggest craft gain per unit of risk; needs a real font decision
Home · above the fold

Same content budget as A, far more of the library visible. Every row carries its own category colour, date and read time — the scent that "GENERAL ×11" destroys today.

Direction C · Divergent

Fact First

Still Explore, but the brand promise leads. Instead of a tagline the masthead states an actual verified fact from the library, with its source and a link to the article — the site demonstrates its value in the first screen rather than describing it. Below, tiles are weighted by editorial importance, not poured into an even grid. Warm paper in light, deep navy field in dark.

Bet
Curiosity is the product — show a fact, don't promise facts
Composition
12-col grid with genuine weight differences; numerals as editorial furniture
Editorial cost
Needs a curated, sourced "fact of the day" per language. Real ongoing work
Risk
Highest. Strongest identity; fails loudly if the fact rotation is ever stale or unsourced
Home · above the fold

The masthead fact is real and attributed to the article it came from. If a fact cannot be sourced it must not run — that constraint is the direction's whole integrity.

The tower's design takes its geometry from the Hymenocallis, a desert flower whose three-lobed form gave engineers a way to buttress a very tall, very slender building against wind.

That shape is not decoration. Each setback as the tower rises interrupts the wind so it cannot organise into a single rhythm strong enough to sway the structure.
